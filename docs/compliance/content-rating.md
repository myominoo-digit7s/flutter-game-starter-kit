# Content Rating Guide

Content ratings are assigned through different store processes. Answer the
current questionnaire for each store using the finished game's content and
features; a rating in one store does not replace the other store's submission.

## Google Play

Complete the app content rating questionnaire in Play Console. Google Play
uses the International Age Rating Coalition (IARC) process to provide ratings
for participating regions. Review the [Google Play content rating
guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898843)
and answer based on the actual app.

## Apple App Store

Complete the age-rating questionnaire in App Store Connect. Apple uses its
own age-rating process and questions; it is separate from Google's IARC
questionnaire. See [Set an app age rating](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/set-an-app-age-rating/)
for Apple's current instructions.

Review the finished game's content and interactive features before submitting
either questionnaire. Ratings and available answers depend on the details you
provide; this guide does not predict a rating or guarantee store approval.
