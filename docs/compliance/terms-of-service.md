# Terms of Service Guide

## Required Placeholders
The Terms of Service relies on:
- `appName`
- `companyName`
- `contactEmail`

## Ads / Rewards Section
If `hasAds` is true, ensure your Terms clarify that any in-game rewards received for watching ads have no real-world monetary value.

## Optional virtual-currency consideration

If the finished game includes virtual currency or an in-game economy, consider
whether game-specific terms should explain purchase, use, expiration, refund,
and transfer rules. These are optional topics to assess, not universal wording
or a legal conclusion; requirements and enforceability depend on the service
and applicable jurisdictions.

## Limitation Disclaimer Placeholder
Ensure the Limitation of Liability section protects your company from damages arising from the use of the app. Consult legal counsel to ensure enforceability in your jurisdiction.
